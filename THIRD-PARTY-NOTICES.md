## Fonts and Microsoft material

The repository contains no font files and no Microsoft code, templates or documents. Where a document asks for a
font the computer doesn't have, the editor's stylesheet refers to a stand-in installed on the computer by name
(`local()`), sized to the original's widths. `src/engine/metrics.json` holds measurements (ascent, descent, line gap
per em) of common fonts, facts about the fonts rather than any part of them.

The test documents in `test/fixtures` are generated by the scripts beside them. The documents the layout was measured
against are private and not in the repository.
